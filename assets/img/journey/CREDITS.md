- `linktop-health-monitor.png`: Linktop's own product photo, sent by Jiatuo as the device used in the IDTS project at
  the Lab of Brain-Computer Intelligence. It is not under an open license; the board credits it as "Linktop (product
  image, supplied by Jiatuo)". Replace it with an openly licensed or self-taken photo if one becomes available.
- `imu-board.png`: SparkFun releases its product photos under CC BY 2.0; source page
  <https://www.sparkfun.com/products/13284>.

  photographed products; no logo is shown on its own.
- `salutatorian-cords.png`: the source photo shows Kappa Pi's purple-and-gold honor cord. It was cut out of its
  background and its purple strands shifted to blue; the gold was kept. It shows cords of the kind, not Jiatuo's own.

  licensed photos found don't give a clean cutout, so the earlier small prints were removed), the Pixel
