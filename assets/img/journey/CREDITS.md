## 3D models

3D models of the artifacts (`model` in `_data/journey.yml`), in `assets/models/journey/` (paths below are relative to
`assets/`). They are shown only in the close-examine view; the board keeps each artifact's cutout. Each was made from a
photo of the object by an image-to-3D model run locally (the sides the photo does not show are inferred), so it is a
derivative of that photo and keeps its license: a model made from a CC BY-SA photo is CC BY-SA, one made from Jiatuo's own
photo is Jiatuo's. Each row names the source photo, the photo's license, and the generator and its license. An artifact whose
model did not look real has no model and keeps only its cutout. The one exception is the Pixel Watch: its model is not
generated but Google's own product model of the Pixel Watch 3, adapted to Jiatuo's watch as the photo shows it. It is
© Google, with no license stated, so it is not openly licensed; it is used at Jiatuo's request, like the Linktop and cord
product images, and Google's marks on it are kept as they are.

No model is shown for the salutatorian cords (the generator gave two flat rings in the wrong colours), the eye
tracker (its only photo is a thin sliver of the bar), the IMU board (every seed, 1-12, gave it extra, ragged mounting
holes and garbled print in the wrong places, so it did not look like the board in its photo) or the 2020 robot; all keep

screen came out a glowing dome and its band a stiff plank); Google's model replaced that try. Settings, seeds and
verdicts for every artifact are in `bin/journey_models.json`, and `bin/build_journey_models.py` rebuilds any of them.
