{% comment %} One document on a "My Journey" board (_includes/journey.html), as a list item; its kind, medium and scan_kind choose how it looks (_sass/_journey-board.scss) and how the close-examine view shows it (assets/js/journey-examine.js). An artifact (kind: artifact) is an object tied to the work. medium "cutout" (the default; a legacy "bag" is drawn the same way) is a picture of the object with a transparent background, lying on the board under its own shadow; "photo" is a small print. Its name and the rest ride in a caption (.board-artifact-caption) that the board hides and the examine view reads; there is no tag, tape or bag. A note is a sticky note, or a notebook page when its medium says so (default: a sticky note for "Next stop", else a notebook page). A doc with a scan_kind is a real document: "diploma" (a scan on heavy cream stock), "paper-page" (a paper's first page as a stapled printout) or "cover" (a closed, padded diploma cover with a plain typed label and nothing else on it); the scan is the doc's scan (or its image when it has no scan), described by scan_alt (or image_alt). Its title and the rest ride on a typed slip under the sheet (.board-scan-caption). A person may carry a portrait (portrait, portrait_alt, portrait_credit, portrait_credit_link): a small print paper-clipped to the file card (.board-portrait). With a portrait_pixel (a mild pre-baked mosaic, bin/build_journey_pixelated.py) the board, the examine view and the JS-free page show that pixelated stand-in; only "Reveal photo" in the examine view shows the photo itself (data-original). Docs with an attach (the id of another doc) are drawn inside that document's item, in a nested list after its text, so they move with it on the board and in a spread pile, and read in place without JavaScript. Called with doc= (the document) and docs= (all of the stop's docs). Liquid assigns are global, so every variable here starts with jd_, and the attached documents come last, after this one's own variables are spent. The text itself is _includes/journey-doc-text.html. Every paper (in a scan, its sheet; not an object) starts with an empty .board-wear carrying its folds and handling (_journey-board.scss): data-crease, data-lift, data-shift, data-edge and data-age, picked from a hash of the doc's id (the same on every build) among those that suit its kind: a letter is folded in three, a print or a diploma's card only waves, a leatherette cover never folds, and
