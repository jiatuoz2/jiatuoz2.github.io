{% comment %} "My Journey": a compact timeline strip, then a split view of two panes side by side, the Map (_includes/journey-map.html, assets/js/journey-map.js) and the Board, one detective board per place (assets/js/journey-board.js), all wired together by assets/js/journey.js; any document on a board opens in the close-examine view (assets/js/journey-examine.js). Content lives in _data/journey.yml. The panes keep fixed shares of the row, the map about a third and the board about two thirds, and never change size on a click (_sass/_journey-layout.scss); "Expand map" opens the map in a window instead. Each board is a dark slate with the place's streets drawn on it (assets/img/journey/maps/, from OpenStreetMap; built by bin/build_journey_maps.py), its papers grouped under strips of yellow masking tape. The board's own title is a plain sheet of paper held at the top of the board by two strips of blue painter's tape (.board-plate inside .board-case): the place name large (the h3, the board's heading), the case file number and the period smaller. The stop's summary sits under it (hidden on the mounted board, read by screen readers) and appears in the examine view of the place. Each document is drawn by _includes/journey-doc.html (an artifact attached to another document is drawn inside that one). Without JavaScript every board is shown in order as plain lists (a stack is a nested list), the timeline links to them, and the Map pane is hidden. {% endcomment %} {% assign stops = site.data.journey.stops %}
