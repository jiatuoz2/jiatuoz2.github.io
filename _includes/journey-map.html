The pane stays hidden without JavaScript, and the globe inside it until the script finds WebGL; the map library and its tiles load as the section nears the viewport. The live day/night readout, the hint and the zoom buttons sit inside the frame. The pane keeps one size beside the board; "Expand map" opens the same live map in a window (.journey-map-window, a modal dialog over the dimmed page) and "Close map", Escape or a click outside puts it back. {% endcomment %}
